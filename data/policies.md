# Ronin Athletics — Policies (Internal)

## MAP (Minimum Advertised Price) Policy
- MAP is enforced on all marketplaces including Amazon. Advertising below MAP is a violation.
- Current MAP: Shogun Gi $129, Tengu Rashguard $49, Kage Shorts $44, Yurei Spats $39, Ranked Belt $19, Core Hoodie $54.
- Promotional exception: coupons up to 15% off MAP are permitted during official sale windows (Black Friday, Prime Day, brand anniversary in July). Anything deeper requires VP Sales sign-off.
- MAP violations by a reseller trigger a 2-strike process: written warning, then supply suspension.

## Return & Refund Policy (DTC + Amazon)
- 30-day returns on unworn items with tags. Gis must be unwashed.
- Amazon FBA returns follow Amazon's standard policy; we do not extend beyond 30 days.
- Defective items (stitching, print peel) are replaced free within 90 days — this is a warranty claim, not a return.
- Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5 business days of receiving the item.

## Warranty
- 90-day workmanship warranty on all gis and rashguards (seams, stitching, sublimation).
- Belts and accessories carry a 30-day warranty.
- Warranty does not cover normal wear, improper washing (hot water / bleach), or competition damage.

## Shipping SLA
- DTC orders ship within 1 business day; 2-5 day delivery in the continental US.
- Amazon FBA handles Prime SLA for FBA ASINs.
- Free DTC shipping over $99.

## Amazon Account Health Guardrails
- Never advertise below MAP on Amazon (protects Buy Box and brand).
- Order Defect Rate (ODR) target < 1%. Late Shipment Rate target < 4%.
- Any listing suppression or policy warning is a P1 incident — escalate to the Amazon channel owner immediately.
