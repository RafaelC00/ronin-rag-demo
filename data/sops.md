# Ronin Athletics — Standard Operating Procedures (Internal)

## SOP-01: Responding to a BSR Drop
When a flagship ASIN's Best Seller Rank worsens (higher number) by more than 3 positions week-over-week:
1. Check whether a competitor changed price or launched a Sponsored placement (see competitor tracker).
2. Verify our Buy Box is intact and price is at MAP (never below).
3. Check inventory — a BSR drop plus low inventory usually means we are being suppressed on availability.
4. If competitor undercut on price and our margin allows, request a coupon (up to 15% off MAP) rather than a MAP change.
5. Post a summary to the #amazon-ops channel and tag the channel owner.

## SOP-02: Listing Optimization Checklist
- Title: brand + product line + key attribute + size/color, under 200 chars.
- Bullets: lead with IBJJF-legal, GSM weight, seam construction, care.
- A+ content refreshed each quarter.
- Backend search terms include competitor brand-adjacent generic terms (never trademarked brand names).

## SOP-03: Review Response Protocol
- Respond to all 1-3 star reviews within 24 hours.
- Defective-product reviews trigger a warranty replacement offer.
- Never dispute a sizing complaint — point to the size chart and offer an exchange.

## SOP-04: Restock Rules
- Reorder trigger: 6 weeks of forward cover based on trailing 4-week velocity.
- Flagship Shogun Gi never goes below 800 units on hand (stockout risk to #1 BSR).
- Belts are high-attach accessories; keep 4+ weeks cover to protect gi conversion.

## SOP-05: Daily Amazon Brief (what the report agent compiles)
Every morning the brief should include: flagship BSR and week-over-week delta, any competitor price/BSR moves, inventory risks (any SKU under reorder trigger), and one recommended action.
