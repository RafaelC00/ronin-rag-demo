# Ronin Athletics — Brand Brief (Internal)

## Positioning
Ronin Athletics is the #1 Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu apparel brand on Amazon US. We make competition-grade, IBJJF-legal gear that a serious hobbyist or competitor can afford. "Masterless warrior, disciplined craft."

## Target Customer
- Core: 24-40 year old BJJ practitioners training 3+ times per week, blue-to-brown belt.
- Secondary: MMA and grappling crossover athletes; gift buyers for practitioners.

## Voice
- Confident, technical, respectful of the art. No hype, no fake scarcity.
- We talk like a training partner who knows their gear, not a salesperson.

## Channels
- Amazon US (primary, ~70% of revenue), Shopify DTC (~25%), wholesale to gyms (~5%).
- Omnichannel: Amazon drives discovery; DTC captures repeat and higher-margin lifestyle line.

## Margin Philosophy
- Protect the #1 BSR on the flagship Shogun Gi at all costs — it is the top of the funnel.
- Defend price with coupons and value, not MAP erosion.
- Grow blended margin via accessories (belts, spats) and the higher-margin Core lifestyle line.
